# What happens when I drift away from making stuff

Hi friend,

I have been on the road a lot lately and I got to meet so many of you in person: Paper World in Orlando, then Anaheim last weekend. It's truly incredible to be able to connect with you all and see your smiling faces. I will never take for granted how excited we all get about our crafty hobbies. I'm also always blown away by what you make with the supplies I dream up. Your creative twist is one of my favorite parts of this dream job.

If I cannot see you in person, the easiest way to spend time with me right now is the videos. The newest one is about what happens when I drift away from making stuff, and the ten things that bring me back. I hand-lettered the list so it can live on your desk, and it is yours free right here: [10 ways to reconnect with your creativity](https://www.amytangerine.com/creativity)

I'm leaning into #2 & #6 this season. How about you?
